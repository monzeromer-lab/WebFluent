Things that will trip you

E106

There is no onClick attribute. A handler is a block: on click { }.

E002

Arguments are separated by commas; statements by new lines — never a comma between statements.

V01

A flag needs its dot after the parentheses: Button("Save").primary.

X01

A derived cannot be assigned. Change what it reads instead.
